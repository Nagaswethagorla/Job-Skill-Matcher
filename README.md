# 🚀 Job Skill Matcher – AI Career Assistant

## 📌 About the Project

**Job Skill Matcher – AI Career Assistant** is an AI-powered web application designed to help job seekers find suitable job opportunities based on their skills, qualifications, and experience.

The platform uses AI to analyze uploaded resumes, extract relevant skills, and compare them with job descriptions to calculate a job compatibility score. It also identifies missing skills and recommends relevant online courses to help candidates improve their professional skills.

Recruiters can use the platform to post job opportunities, search candidate profiles, evaluate skill compatibility, and manage applications efficiently.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many job applicants apply for jobs without knowing whether their skills match the job requirements. At the same time, recruiters spend considerable time reviewing resumes that may not be relevant to their job openings.

This platform addresses these challenges through automated resume analysis, intelligent job matching, skill gap identification, and candidate recommendations.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Applicant registers and logs into the platform.
2. Applicant uploads a PDF or DOCX resume.
3. AI analyzes the resume and extracts skills, education, and experience.
4. The extracted information is compared with available job descriptions.
5. The system calculates a job match percentage.
6. Matched and missing skills are displayed.
7. Relevant courses are recommended to bridge skill gaps.
8. Applicants can apply for suitable jobs.
9. Recruiters can search and evaluate candidates based on their requirements.

## 🔐 Application Constraints

- Maximum 10 job applications per applicant per day.
- Duplicate applications are prevented.
- Resume files are stored securely.
- Role-based access control for applicants and recruiters.
- Job and candidate search is optimized using database queries and indexes.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective is to simplify the recruitment process by connecting job seekers with suitable opportunities through AI-powered resume analysis and skill-based job recommendations.

The platform aims to reduce irrelevant job applications, save recruiters' time, and help applicants improve their employability through personalized learning recommendations.
